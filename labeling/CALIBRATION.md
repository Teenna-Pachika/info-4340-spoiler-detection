## Rubric (Week 7, unchanged)

| Dimension | 4 | 3 | 2 | 1 | 0 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Correct call** | Clearly right; anyone would agree | Right, but the review is borderline | A toss-up | Probably wrong | Clearly wrong |
| **Evidence** | Names the exact plot detail, or correctly says it's only opinions | Right part, but vague | Generic; could fit almost any review | Points to the wrong part | No reason, or it contradicts the answer |
| **Faithfulness** | Everything is in the review | Small paraphrase, nothing wrong | One claim a bit exaggerated or off | A claim the review doesn't support | Makes up plot details |
| **Usefulness** | Moderator decides right away | Mostly; a quick glance | Reread part of it | Reread all of it | Not usable |

New for the final: an answer of **Unsure** gets correct_call 3–4 only if the review is genuinely ambiguous.
